Regime Sleeve

A rules-based cross-asset rotation: hold the broad US market (rSPY) in calm uptrends, rotate into Bitcoin, gold, or cash when the trend breaks. One holding at a time, with a hysteresis buffer so it does not flip on noise. This is a backtest — no orders are sent.

The rule — alpha logic

Frozen in internal/strategy/spec.go. Every parameter below is read from the backtest's Spec, so it always matches the code that ran.

  • Decide on yesterday's close only — today's close is the result, never an input.
  • Hold the broad US market (rSPY) when it is above its {{.Result.Spec.EquitySMA}}-day average and realized vol is under {{share .Result.Spec.VolStress}}/yr.
  • Otherwise hold Bitcoin if it is above its {{.Result.Spec.AltSMA}}-day average and the stock trend is not that calm uptrend.
  • Otherwise hold gold (PAXG) if it is above its {{.Result.Spec.AltSMA}}-day average — else hold cash.
  • Hysteresis: clear the trend line by {{share .Result.Spec.Band}} to switch on, drop only when price falls back below it, and hold at least {{.Result.Spec.MinHold}} trading days. Over this test that meant just {{num .Result.Full.Strategy.Turnover}} switches a year.
  • Weekends are not tradable — a Friday holding is marked through Monday's close.
  • Each switch costs {{bps .Result.Spec.CostRate}} (fee + slippage). A harsher {{bps .Result.HarshCost}} run is in the score sheet below.

The rule (alpha logic)

Frozen in internal/strategy/spec.go. One holding at a time, decided on yesterday's close. The numbers below are read straight from the backtest's Spec, so they always match the code that ran.

  • Look at yesterday's close only. Today's close is the result, not an input.
  • Hold the broad US market token (rSPY) when it is above its {{.Result.Spec.EquitySMA}}-day average and recent swings are under {{share .Result.Spec.VolStress}} a year.
  • Otherwise hold Bitcoin if its price is above the {{.Result.Spec.AltSMA}}-day average and the stock trend is not that calm uptrend.
  • Otherwise hold gold if gold is above its {{.Result.Spec.AltSMA}}-day average. Otherwise hold cash.
  • A holding must clear its trend line by {{share .Result.Spec.Band}} to switch on, only drops when price falls back below the line, and is held at least {{.Result.Spec.MinHold}} trading days. Across this test that kept switching down to {{num .Result.Full.Strategy.Turnover}} times a year.
  • Saturday and Sunday are not treated as days you can trade. A Friday holding is scored through Monday's close.
  • Each change of holding costs {{bps .Result.Spec.CostRate}} (a fee plus a small slippage allowance). A harsher {{bps .Result.HarshCost}} run is in the score sheet.

Words on this page (plain-English glossary)
Sharpe
The score judges use. It is the profit divided by how bumpy the path was. Around 1 is a solid path. It is not a forecast.
Held-out days
The last {{.Result.OOSDays}} weekdays were not used to design the rule. They are the check that the older result was not a fluke.
rSPY
Bitget's token of the S&P 500 (the SPY ETF). It is the stock holding when the market trend is healthy. The sector tokens are loaded too, but this frozen rule holds the broad market rather than chasing a sector.
PAXG
A token that tracks gold. That is the gold holding.
Update prices
Downloads the candles again. The first open, and every click of that button, can take a minute. A normal refresh of the page uses the prices already saved.
